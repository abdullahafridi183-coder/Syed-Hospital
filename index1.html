Disciplines under one roof: psychiatry, psychotherapy, family therapy

Care that treats the person, not only the diagnosis

The hospital was founded by Prof. Dr. Sayed Mohammad Sultan, MRCPsych (London), FRCPsych, and former President of the Pakistan Psychiatric Society. His approach paired careful psychiatric assessment with talk therapy and family involvement — never medication as the only answer.

That approach continues today. Every new patient starts with a full assessment before any treatment plan is proposed, and families are welcomed into the process wherever it helps rather than treated as bystanders.

Where to start

Most people aren't sure which service fits. A first consultation is where that gets sorted out — these are the paths it can lead to.

Psychiatric assessment

A full clinical evaluation for anxiety, depression, mood disorders, psychosis and other conditions, leading to a diagnosis and treatment plan — medication included only where it genuinely helps.

Individual psychotherapy

Talk therapy for adults working through anxiety, low mood, trauma, grief or life transitions, at a pace set by the person in the room, not a fixed programme.

Family therapy

Sessions that bring parents, partners or siblings into the room together, used when a person's difficulties are tangled up with what's happening at home.

Adolescent and child mental health

Assessment and therapy adapted for younger patients, with parents brought in from the first session rather than kept at arm's length.

Follow-up and medication review

Ongoing appointments for anyone already on a treatment plan, to review how medication or therapy is working and adjust it as things change.
